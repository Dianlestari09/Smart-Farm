🔧 Hardware & Database

Mikrokontroler ESP32 DevKit V1
Sensor Suhu & Kelembapan SHT31 (I2C SDA 21, SCL 22)
Sensor Gas Amonia MQ-135 (ADC GPIO 34)
Aktuator PWM (MOSFET) GPIO 16 (In), 17 (Ex), 23 (Heat)
Aktuator Relai Biner GPIO 27 (Motor Konveyor)
Cloud Database Tabel Tunggal: 'telemetry'

📐 Parameter Standar Kenyamanan Ayam

Suhu Ideal 20.0 °C – 25.0 °C
Kelembapan Ideal 60.0 % – 80.0 %
Batas Aman Gas Amonia < 20.0 ppm
Batas Bahaya Amonia ≥ 20.0 ppm
Pemicu Konveyor Amonia ≥ 20 ppm ATAU RH > 90%

🧠 Parameter Algoritma Fuzzy

Metode FLC Mamdani (Max-Min)
Jumlah Aturan (Rule Base) 125 Aturan (5 × 5 × 5)
Himpunan Input 5 Suhu × 5 RH × 5 Amonia
Defuzzifikasi Centroid COA Domain [0..100]
Scaling PWM Persamaan 3.2 (8.3 - 91.7)
